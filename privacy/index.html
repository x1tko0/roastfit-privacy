RoastFit

Calorie Tracker — Privacy Policy

Short version

What you eat stays on your phone. RoastFit has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission — the food database is built into the app.

Data the app stores

Only what you enter yourself:

  • Food diary: meals, foods, portions, calories and macros, water intake, cheat days.
  • Profile used to calculate your daily limit: goal, age, height, weight, activity level and the formula you choose (female/male metabolism formula).
  • Optional weight log, intermittent fasting plan, chosen ready-made menu, your own foods and meals, your own roast lines, the name or nickname you choose for roasts, and app settings.

This data:

  • is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage;
  • is never sent to the developer or any third party;
  • is deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Health information

Weight, height, age and what you eat are health-related. RoastFit uses them only on your device to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets and to show your own statistics. The app is not a medical device and gives no medical advice; it never sells, shares or uploads this information.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include RoastFit's data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by Android and Google (Google Privacy Policy), not by us, and you can turn it off in system settings.
  • Backup file and CSV. “Save backup” and “Export CSV” create a file only where you choose to save it. We never see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, camera, photos or files (except a backup file you pick to import).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app on your phone:

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “RoastFit Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details.
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by Google Play.

Notifications

Meal reminders, the evening summary, water reminders and fasting window notifications are scheduled on your device. Notification text can appear on your lock screen — you can turn any of them off in Settings.

Sharing

“Share the shame” creates an image on your device and shares it only when you tap it and choose where to send it.

Age

RoastFit is intended for adults (18+). It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
